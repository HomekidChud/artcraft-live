      Paired devices
      
            
      
            
      To pair: install the ChatCraft APK, open it, and point it at . The app posts to POST /api/phone/pair and starts polling — it appears here within a minute of being opened.
      
            
      On the phone, enable the ChatCraft accessibility service (the APK links you to the right settings screen) so the agent can see the screen and tap for you. No root needed; iOS does not allow this.
